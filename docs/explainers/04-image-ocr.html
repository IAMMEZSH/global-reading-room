Image Intelligence & OCR

Article images are proxied, cached and OCR-ready. Recognize the text inside a chart, screenshot or infographic with the Windows OCR engine or a vision model, then translate it line by line.

Cached, guarded image proxy

Remote images are fetched once, validated against private-network addresses, stored under data/images and served from localhost.

Two OCR engines

Windows uses the built-in WinRT OCR engine with no extra install. A vision model can be configured for other systems or harder layouts.

Read, then translate

The panel keeps the recognized source text separate from the Chinese translation, so OCR errors stay visible and checkable.

Zoom to verify

Any article image opens in a full-screen lightbox with wheel zoom, drag-to-pan and a one-click reset — ideal for dense charts.
